OFFER AGREEMENT

for the provision of leisure services

This version is effective from 1 September 2026.

AurinkoLab ry
Espoo, Finland

1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

1.1. This offer agreement (hereinafter the "Agreement") is the official public offer of the non-profit organization AurinkoLab ry (hereinafter the "Provider") to conclude an agreement for the provision of leisure services on the terms set out below.

1.2. Acceptance of this offer is the attendance of classes and/or the payment by the Participant for the Provider's services.

1.3. A Participant (hereinafter the "Participant") is an individual who has accepted the terms of this Agreement by performing the actions specified in clause 1.2.

1.4. By attending classes, the Participant automatically agrees to all the terms of this Agreement.

2. SUBJECT OF THE AGREEMENT

2.1. The Provider undertakes to provide the Participant with leisure services in the form of group/individual classes in subjects (disciplines) — clubs, workshops, sections, etc. — and the Participant undertakes to pay for these services in the manner and on the terms set out in this Agreement.

2.2. The specific list of subjects, the class schedule, and the pricing are determined by the Provider and published on the website / in the organization's information materials.

3. PRICING AND PAYMENT

3.1. Annual membership

3.1.1. The cost of tuition for one subject is €500 per academic year (the academic year begins on 1 September).

3.1.2. Payment is made on a prepayment basis by one of the following methods: a single payment for the whole academic year or by semester (€250 per semester).

3.1.3. When paying by semester, a fee of €10 is charged for each additional invoice issued (per new invoice).

3.1.4. Payment must be made no later than the first class of the period being paid for.

3.1.5. The annual membership price is calculated for the published teaching weeks and accounts for holidays.

3.2. Single visit

3.2.1. A single visit to an individual class without an annual membership is €20 per class and is paid before the class begins.

3.3. Discounts

3.3.1. If a single Participant attends two subjects, a discount of 30% on the second subject is provided.

3.3.2. If several children from the same family attend classes, a discount of 30% on the tuition of the second child is provided.

4. SCHEDULE AND HOLIDAYS

4.1. Classes are held during the published teaching weeks; specific days depend on the subject. Classes are not held during Espoo school holidays.

  • Weeks of 2026: 36 to 52, except week 42.
  • Weeks of 2027: 2 to 21, except week 8.
  • Christmas break: 23 Dec 2026 – 6 Jan 2027.

4.3. Public holidays: Good Friday 26 Mar 2027, Easter Monday 29 Mar 2027, May Day (Vappu) 1 May 2027, Ascension Day 6 May 2027.

5. ABSENCES AND RECALCULATION

5.1. If a Participant misses a class on their own initiative, no recalculation is made, except in cases of prolonged illness (>2 weeks) with a medical certificate.

5.2. If a class is cancelled by the Provider, a recalculation is made or an additional class is provided.

By taking part in classes and/or paying for the services, the Participant confirms that they have read the terms of this Offer Agreement, understand them, and accept them in full.
