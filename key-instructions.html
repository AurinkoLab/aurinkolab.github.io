The Makasiini key

Ana hands out the key box code to anyone on the list. Take the key whenever you need it, session or no session. What matters is checking in when you arrive and out when you leave. To find out who has it right now, send /key.

Taking the key

When you arrive

  1. Send /checkin to Ana Works at any hour. You don’t need a booked session for this.
  2. Tap the shaded strip in her reply The code sits underneath, so it stays out of notifications on a locked screen.
  3. Open the box and take the key The same message tells you where the box is.
  4. Close the box and spin the dials A code left standing on the wheels is readable by anyone walking past.
  5. Tap «I have the key» One tap, and everyone can see the key is with you.

Bringing it back

When you leave

  1. Tidy up first Sink empty and clean, ground-floor work table clear.
  2. Send /checkout Ana walks you through it — just answer in the chat.
  3. Photograph the kitchen and sink Take it on your phone and send it straight to her.
  4. Photograph the ground-floor work table Second photo, and the last one.
  5. Put the key back, spin the dials Do this before tapping the button, not after, or it gets forgotten.
  6. Tap «Key is back, leaving» Both photos go to the group chat with the time on them.
Ana reminds you about the key 10 minutes before your session ends. If there is still no checkout half an hour later, she says so in the group chat — not to catch anyone out, but so that somebody living nearby can go and try the door.

When something goes wrong

Ana says you have no access
Your number is in that same message — forward it to Maria and she can let you in without restarting anything.
The key isn’t in the box
Send /key. She will tell you who has it and since when.
The code doesn’t work
Message Maria. The code changes now and then, and Ana always has the current one.
You forgot to check out
Send /checkout as soon as you remember. The photos are still wanted, even after the fact.
You left without closing the bot
No harm done. The key stays logged to you until you tap the return button.
